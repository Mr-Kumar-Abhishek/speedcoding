2. SpeedCo I Memory, 72-Bit Word Layouts & Arithmetic Rules

Electrostatic & Auxiliary Memory

  • Electrostatic CRT Store: 1024 words (0000..=1023).
  • User Storage: 0300..=1013 (714 words); execution starts at PC = 0300.
  • Control Storage: 0000..=0299 & 1014..=1023 protected (0025 holds R-control state).
  • Drums P & Q: 1024 words each (0..=1023, no wrap).
  • Tapes J..M: Variable unit records, 6 data + 1 odd-parity redundancy channel + checksum.

Arithmetic & Set 1 Math Rules

  • Decimal Exponent Limit: 10-digit fraction with |E10| ≤ 236; binary |E2| ≤ 131,071.
  • Add/Sub Restrictions: Decimal exponent difference > 76 rejected; exact cancellation keeps F = 0 and operand exponent without shifting.
  • Truncation vs Rounding: Multiply/add/sub are unrounded; DIV/NGDIV form 34/35-bit rounded quotients; F(B)=0 stops with DivideCheck.
  • Set 1 Quirk Rules: SQRT negative/zero F yields F=0, exp ⌈E/2⌉; SINE zero F yields exp +7; LN treats negative as |x| and zero F as 1/2.

4. 80-Column Cards, Tape Status Matrix, Checking, Listing & Timing

80-Column Card Formats (Section 7)

  • Instruction Card: Col 8 not 0+1, Col 9 blank, 10–13 Location, 14=0, 15–19 Alpha OP1, 20 R, 21–32 A/B/C, 33–34=00, 35–39 Alpha OP2, 40–43 D, 44 L, 46–51 Num OP1/OP2, 52–55=0000.
  • Data Card: Col 8 double-punched 0+1, Col 9=8, up to 5 decimal floats (Col 45 skipped in N3; 12=+, 11=- overpunch), Col 80 word count 1..=5.
  • Loading Control Card: Col 9=9, 11–14 first, 16–19 last, 21–24 drum addr, 29 unit (J..M or P/Q).

Checking, Listing & Section 12 Timing

  • Checking Loop (Sec. 10): Pass 1 sums fixed-point words in Counter 1; Pass 2 sums in Counter 2 and skips on match. PRINT suppressed on Pass 2 (2.896 ms).
  • Selective Listing (Sec. 11): Switches 1/2/3 match L=0/3/6; snapshots post-OP1/pre-OP2; flushes every 10 lines (1.325s avg / 1.370s max); |E|>236 prints ±999.
  • Tape Length & Loading (Sec. 12): Length = 2*72 + sum((n+0.5)*3/25) + (k-1) in at 75 in/s; EOF gap = 0.96s; load = 0.4n + 0.6c + e (e=2 or 55).
